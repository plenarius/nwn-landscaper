- **The ground has relief in the game's light.** The tiles' shaders tilt the normal the light reads by the slope of the
  texture's brightness, taken as height: on a library texture along each of its three projections, on a tile's baked picture
  over its square (the new `ls_bk_fs`). Stones of gravel, cobbles and rock take a lit side and a shaded one under a torch or
  the sun, with nothing more in the hak. Lit per vertex (the game's Minimal shaders), nothing changes. See
  [Light](../docs/handbook/light.md#relief-on-the-ground).
