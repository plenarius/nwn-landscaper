- **A canvas made from an area exports that area back.** A canvas header may name `tagPrefix`, what every tag the engine gives
  in its pieces starts with (else the canvas's name), so a canvas made from an area keeps that area's door, object and
  transition tags. A piece carries its own `originals` (an imported area's own objects, as changed), and what stands in a
  piece is moved into its metres in double precision, to the nanometre, so a piece that is a whole area gives back its
  numbers. Exporting the lsplains showcase as itself and as the only piece of a canvas made from it gives the same report,
  heights, paint, water, objects and area header. See [the handbook's page](../docs/handbook/canvases.md).
- **Canvases in the docs:** [a handbook page](../docs/handbook/canvases.md) for making, sculpting, cutting and exporting a
  canvas (the editor's draft from Pieces mode, moved here), and
  canvases in [the builder's path](../docs/BUILDER-WORKFLOW.md) and [the team server](../docs/TEAM-SERVER.md).
