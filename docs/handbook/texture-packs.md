# Texture packs

A texture pack adds ready-made textures to the library, in their own categories. Like the rest of the library, they go into every
export's texture hak, `ls_textures.hak`.

## Install a pack

1. Pick **Paint** (`5`) and click **Get more textures**.
2. The list shows each pack with its licence, its credits and a few swatches.
3. Click **Install**. The pack downloads, and its textures join their categories in the Paint panel.

**Remove** takes a pack out again, unless an area still paints with one of its textures.

![The texture packs dialog](../assets/handbook/texture-packs.webp)

!!! note "On a team server"
    The library is the whole team's, so only admins install or remove packs.
