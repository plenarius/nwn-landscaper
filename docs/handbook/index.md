# The handbook

How to build an area in the Landscaper, one task a page. Read [Getting started](getting-started.md) first; after that, each page
stands on its own.

The order follows the work on an area:

1. **[Getting started](getting-started.md):** start the program, make a new area or import one, move the view.
2. **[Terrain](terrain.md):** raise, lower, smooth and flatten the ground; water, chasms and cave mouths.
3. **[Paint and the mix brush](paint.md):** paint the ground with one texture, or with a mix of several in patches.
4. **[Caves and interiors](caves.md):** dig passages, cut rooms, lay ramps and whole descents.
5. **[Scatter and Place](scatter.md):** trees, rocks and bushes, painted on or set one by one.
6. **[Things](things.md):** creatures, doors, transitions, triggers and the rest, with their settings.
7. **[Light](light.md):** the area's sun and moon, and each tile's two lights.
8. **[Ambience](ambience.md):** mist, fireflies, leaves, light shafts and the other particles, baked into the tiles.
9. **[Atmosphere and skies](atmosphere.md):** light and fog presets, sky boxes, sky packs and Real Skies.
10. **[Texture packs](texture-packs.md):** more textures to paint with.
11. **[Export](export.md):** the area into the game, and the path check.
12. **[Publish and teams](publish.md):** hand the area over, alone or on a team server.

[The whole path](../BUILDER-WORKFLOW.md) is the same work on one page, with every rule and limit.
